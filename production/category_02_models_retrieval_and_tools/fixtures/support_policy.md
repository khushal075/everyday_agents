# Sample support policy

This fictional policy is used in the Everyday Agent retrival exercise.

## Duplicate Charges

If a customer was charged twice for the same subscription, send the case
to billing for review. Include both transaction IDs when available.
Billing confirms whether the payments are duplicates before issuing a refund.

## Refund requests

If a customer asks for a refund after cancelling, check the cancellation
date and the payments date. Send the case to billing sif the customer made
the payment after cancellation.

## Account Access

If a customer cannot sign in, direct them to the password reset page.
IF they no longer have access to the email address on the account, send the case to
account support for identity verification.
